Company Profile

A local IT company built on two decades of infrastructure work.

BUMI IT Consulting Sdn Bhd was founded in April 2000 by a group of highly skilled professionals. The company is a leading local Bumiputera IT company that supports the government's efforts to promote greater Bumiputera participation in the IT industry.

As a 100% high-technology company, BUMI IT is devoted to delivering complete solutions and value-added services to corporate clients, with a specific emphasis on the government and education sectors. The team works closely with clients to understand their operating environment and design IT infrastructure that holds up to real-world demands.

From uninterruptible power supply upkeep to network cabling, data centre builds and ongoing IT support, BUMI IT's work sits at the infrastructure layer that government offices, universities and enterprises in Sabah depend on every day.
